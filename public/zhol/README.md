# Portal zhol assets

Source: the owner's supplied `zhol-style-kit.zip` and `STYLE_PROMPT.md`.

- `bg_cream.jpg` and `bg_dark.jpg` are the supplied paper textures, unchanged.
- `map-pencil-320.webp` and `map-pencil-640.webp` are responsive derivatives of `map_pencil_web.png`, retaining transparency.
- `icon-msg.webp` is the supplied `icon_msg.png`, resized to 80px.
- `fonts/` contains the supplied Inter Tight 400, 500 and 600 WOFF2 subsets (Latin, extended Latin, Cyrillic and extended Cyrillic). The accompanying OFL license is retained.

The portal uses these local assets without external font requests or image-generation services. The zhol wordmark is not used; iItaly keeps its own branding. Only assets used by the portal are shipped.
