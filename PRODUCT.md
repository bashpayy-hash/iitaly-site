# iItaly

A Russian-language student workspace for applicants from Kazakhstan planning study in Italy. Next.js static export on Netlify, React client workflows, separate Railway API. Local questionnaire and guest workspace do not authenticate or grant paid access. Cloud sign-in, verified document reviews, task dependencies, Stripe checkout, university data and printed Permesso geometry retain their existing contracts.

The owner cancelled the Renaissance gallery redesign. Preserve the warm UI from commit 1d9b9dc, while retaining the requested usability and content changes. Marketing pages persuade; guides lead sequential reading; the portal supports completing tasks. No decorative assistant portraits. Students own their tasks; legal consent requirements use “законный представитель”. Never invent dates, approval, scholarship awards or admission probabilities. Product previews are explicitly examples.
